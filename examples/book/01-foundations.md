## The unit circle {#sec-circle}

Every periodic signal can be traced back to motion around a circle. A point
sweeping the unit circle at a constant rate projects onto a sine wave — the
single most important picture in this book.

We define the fundamental relationship in @eq-euler, and we will lean on it in
every chapter that follows, including the synthesis argument of @sec-synthesis.

$$ e^{i\theta} = \cos\theta + i\sin\theta $$ (eq-euler)

## Why this matters {#sec-why}

Hold onto @eq-euler. The moment it feels obvious, the rest of the book — the
frequency view in the next chapter, and the reconstruction in the last — will
feel inevitable rather than magical.
