## From time to frequency {#sec-transform}

The transform is a change of coordinates, nothing more. It takes the circular
motion of @eq-euler from the previous chapter and re-expresses a signal as a sum
of such motions, each with its own rate.

## Reading a spectrum {#sec-spectrum}

A spectrum is a bar chart of "how much of each frequency." Once you can read one,
you can read them all — and the synthesis in @sec-synthesis is just this chart,
run backwards.
